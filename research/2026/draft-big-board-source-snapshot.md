# 2026 Fantasy Draft Research · Source Snapshot

## Source

Google Sheet `Fantasy Football Draft Big Board`, tab `2026` (`DRIVE-FFF-DRAFT-BIG-BOARD-2026`).

The observed sheet contains a 2025 NFL receiving-data research table used as input for 2026 fantasy draft evaluation. Fields observed include player, season, team, routes run, receptions, receiving yards, target share, touchdowns, YAC, aDOT, catch rate, total EPA, targets/route and YPRR, plus a second separation-oriented block (e.g. CUSH, SEP, TAY/TAY%, REC/TAR, YAC/R, xYAC/R).

Examples at the top of the observed ranking include Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Puka Nacua, George Pickens, Ja'Marr Chase and Amon-Ra St. Brown.

## Semantics

- NFL performance metrics are **research inputs**, not Fantasy Football Fever league state.
- Rankings/ordering are a **draft-research artifact**, not canonical NFL player ranking truth.
- Source remains authoritative for the original board; this file preserves structure, purpose and provenance without duplicating the entire spreadsheet.
